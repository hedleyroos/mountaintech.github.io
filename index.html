Invest with confidence

Technical due diligence is a crucial part of investing in any startup. Yet technology and software are often overlooked as investors struggle to see through the smoke, mirrors and technical jargon. Our team of engineering experts scratches beneath the surface to make sure that the products you invest in are built by a top team using strong software and technology.

Trust in our process

We dig deeper than the traditional due diligence requires, taking the time to understand how the product works. We assess the architecture, read the source code, witness deployments and discuss the decisions made by the developers. We get the unfiltered truth from the people behind the scenes.
